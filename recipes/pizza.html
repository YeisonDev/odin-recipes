Prepare Your Own Pizza

Pizza Recipe

Here you'll find the best way to prepare your own delicious Homemade Pizza with the most delicious and fresh ingredients out there.

Get ready to taste the best italian-ish homemade pizza made by your own hands with the ingredients below.

Ingredients

For the Dough

  • Warm Water: 1.25 cups (100–110°F/38-43°C)
  • Yeast: 2 tsp instant or active dry yeast
  • Sugar: 1 tbsp (helps activate yeast)
  • Flour: 3 cups bread flour or all-purpose flour
  • Olive Oil: 3 tbsp
  • Salt: 1 tsp
  • Optional: 1/4 tsp garlic powder

For the Sauce & Toppings

  • Sauce: 3/4 cup tomato sauce (or canned crushed tomatoes/passata)
  • Herbs: 1.5 tbsp Italian seasoning, salt to taste
  • Cheese: 1.5–2 cups grated low-moisture mozzarella (fresh mozzarella can make the pizza soggy)
  • Optional Toppings: Pepperoni, sliced mushrooms, black olives, fresh basil
  • Finish: Extra virgin olive oil for drizzling

Steps

  1. Activate Yeast: In a large bowl, combine warm water, sugar, and yeast. Let it sit for 5–10 minutes until it becomes foamy.
  2. Make the Dough: Add the olive oil, salt, and flour to the yeast mixture. Mix until a shaggy dough forms.
  3. Knead: Transfer the dough to a floured surface and knead for 5–7 minutes until smooth and elastic. If using a stand mixer, use the dough hook for 3–5 minutes.
  4. Rise: Place the dough in an oiled bowl, cover it with a warm, damp cloth or plastic wrap, and let it rise in a warm spot for 1 hour or until doubled in size.
  5. Preheat & Prepare: Preheat your oven to its highest setting—usually 450–500°F (230–260°C). If using a pizza stone, place it in the oven while heating.
  6. Shape the Dough: Divide the dough into two balls for thinner crusts. On a floured surface, stretch or roll the dough into a 12-inch circle. Leave the edges slightly thicker for the crust.
  7. Add Toppings: Place dough on a parchment-lined pan (or pizza peel with cornmeal). Spread a thin layer of sauce, top with cheese, and add desired toppings.
  8. Bake: Bake for 10–15 minutes, until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is bubbly and slightly browned.
